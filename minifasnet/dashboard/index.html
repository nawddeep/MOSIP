How to demo (2 minutes)

  1. Show the green Self-check passed pill: the exact verified model files are loaded and give the expected answers.
  2. Start the live camera and look at it: the box turns green — LIVE PERSON.
  3. Hold up a printed photo or a phone showing a face: it should turn red — SPOOF.
  4. Move the threshold: higher = stricter (more spoofs caught, more real people asked to retry).
  5. Point to the chart: the decision is stable over time, not a lucky frame.

Rehearse once in the demo room: lighting, distance (face fills ~¼ of the frame) and camera quality change the scores. This is a passive model only: it does not run blink/smile challenges and is not tuned for this camera yet.
